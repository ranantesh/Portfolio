A Space for Mindful Connection

Prana Yoga is more than a studio; it is a retreat from the noise of London. We believe that true wellness is found when the body, mind, and environment are in perfect harmony.

Our minimalist, light-filled spaces are designed to strip away distractions, allowing you to focus entirely on your breath and movement. With small class sizes and expert guidance, your practice will deepen like never before.
